## How Supercheck Fits Together

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    Detect["<b>Detect</b><br/>Monitors, jobs, tests"] --> Triage["<b>Triage</b><br/>Alerts & incidents"]
    Triage --> Investigate["<b>Investigate</b><br/>Read-only AI SRE"]
    Investigate --> Explain["<b>Explain</b><br/>Cited brief & report"]
    Explain --> Confirm["<b>Confirm Recovery</b><br/>Re-run existing checks"]

    style Detect fill:#eef2ff,stroke:#4338ca,color:#3730a3
    style Triage fill:#fffbeb,stroke:#b45309,color:#92400e
    style Investigate fill:#f5f3ff,stroke:#6d28d9,color:#5b21b6
    style Explain fill:#f0f9ff,stroke:#0369a1,color:#075985
    style Confirm fill:#ecfdf5,stroke:#047857,color:#065f46
```

1. **Detect** with monitors, jobs, and tests.
2. **Triage** from alerts, then open an incident when the signal needs investigation.
3. **Investigate** with native evidence and optional read-only connectors.
4. **Explain** in the incident brief and investigation report.
5. **Confirm recovery** by re-running the same checks. AI SRE does not apply fixes.
