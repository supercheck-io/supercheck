## Quickstart

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open a workspace

Explore the [live demo](https://demo.supercheck.dev) with sample data, or [self-host Supercheck](/docs/app/deployment/self-hosted) with Docker Compose. Supercheck Cloud is coming soon.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a test

Write a browser, API, database, or performance test in the [Playground](/docs/app/automate/playground), or record one with the [Recorder](/docs/recorder).

</Step>
<Step>

### Run it on a schedule

Add the test to a [job](/docs/app/automate/jobs) with a cron schedule. To keep checking production, attach a Playwright test to a [synthetic monitor](/docs/app/monitor#synthetic-monitor). Add HTTP, website, ping, or port monitors for uptime.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get alerted

Add a [notification channel](/docs/app/communicate/alerts) such as email, Slack, or Teams, and select it on your monitors and jobs.

</Step>
<Step>

### Tell your users

Publish a [status page](/docs/app/communicate/status-pages) and link your monitors to its components.

</Step>
</Steps>

To manage all of this as code instead, start with the [CLI](/docs/cli).

## Your account

Open **Account** from the user menu to change your name or profile photo. Your email is read-only. Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP photo up to 10 MB; Supercheck crops it to a square and shrinks it. Photos are visible only to people who share an organization with you.
