Copilot is Supercheck's read-only operational assistant. Access it from **Investigate → Copilot** for open-ended queries or via the floating action panel on any incident page for incident-scoped follow-ups.

![Copilot Chat](/screenshots/sre-copilot-chat.png)

## Chat Modes

| Source | Mode | Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Standalone** | Platform chat (`/copilot`) | Uses user-provided context to explain architectures, craft queries, or analyze pasted error messages. |
| **Incident-scoped** | Incident panel | Directly queries saved incident evidence (metrics, logs, traces, synthetic test artifacts) with native citations. |
| **Live connectors** | Incident chat (opt-in) | Read-only queries to Kubernetes, Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, GitHub, Loki, and other configured connectors. |

<Callout type="info">
Copilot is strictly read-only. It never modifies infrastructure or provider data, applies remediation, or deploys code. It synthesizes findings and suggests safe verification commands.
</Callout>

## Conversation Management

- **New chat** — Starts a clean conversation thread.
- **History** — Search and restore previous triage sessions.
- **Archive** — Archives concluded sessions while preserving audit logs.
- **Chat help** — Provides suggested questions and guidance on what Copilot can use.

Conversations are automatically scoped to your current organization, project, and user identity.

## Investigation Map

For complex outages involving distributed microservices, open the **Investigation Map** (`/copilot/evidence-graph`) to navigate relationships between alerts, incidents, and upstream/downstream services.

![Investigation Map](/screenshots/sre-investigation-map.png)

The map displays:
- Relationships between alert signals, incidents, and the services they affect
- Service topology dependencies and tier classifications
- Interactive node inspection for immediate evidence drill-down

## Live Sources

Live connector queries are **disabled by default**. To activate them during an incident chat:

1. Ensure the incident is linked to a registered service with configured connectors.
2. Verify that your user role has connector investigation permissions (`project_admin` or higher).
3. Toggle **Live sources** in the chat composer before submitting your prompt.

### Safety Guardrails

- **Bounded queries** — Maximum 10 rows, 15-second execution timeout, and a historical time window of up to six hours (60 minutes by default).
- **Namespace isolation** — Kubernetes queries strictly require explicit namespace scoping.
- **Prompt context** — Each message is limited to 4,000 characters. Saved history lets you reopen conversations; previous messages are not automatically included in the model prompt. Restate relevant context in follow-up questions.
- **Model budget** — 4 model steps and a 45-second timeout per answer. The answer is saved when the stream finishes; stopped answers are not saved.
- **Rate limiting** — 30 messages per minute per user. If Redis is unavailable, requests fail closed rather than running unthrottled.
- **Redaction** — All credentials, tokens, and sensitive headers are redacted before reasoning steps.

<Callout type="info">
For a full investigation with a saved, shareable report, use [Incident Investigation](/docs/app/investigate/incidents). Copilot is for interactive questions; an investigation runs the complete evidence pass and records its findings on the incident timeline.
</Callout>
