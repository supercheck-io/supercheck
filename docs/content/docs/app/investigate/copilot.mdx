Open **Investigate → Copilot** to ask about your services, errors, or logs. From an incident, select **Open Copilot** to bring its saved evidence into the same chat screen.

Copilot is read-only: it can gather evidence and suggest checks, but cannot change your systems or apply a fix.

## Live Sources

**Live sources** is on by default for Project Admins and organization admins or owners. Copilot can query configured [connectors](/docs/app/investigate/connectors) when it needs fresh data.

- **General chat** — Name the service you want to check. Copilot finds available sources in your current project and asks if the service is unclear.
- **Incident chat** — Uses the incident's saved evidence and connectors for its linked service. **New chat** keeps this incident context.

Turn **Live sources** off to use conversation text and available saved evidence only. Project Editors can use Copilot without live queries; Viewers cannot use Copilot.

If live data is unavailable, check the service and connector configuration in **Services**. A failed or incomplete query is an evidence gap, not a verified result.

## Conversations

- **New chat** starts a fresh thread. To leave an incident context, open **Investigate → Copilot**.
- **History** searches and restores your saved general and incident chats.
- **Archive** removes a chat from active history.
- **Chat help** offers suggested questions and explains available sources.

History is private to you within your current organization and project. Let a response finish to save it; stopped answers are not saved. Follow-ups use the conversation text. General-chat connector results are temporary and are not added to the incident evidence graph; incident-chat results are saved on the incident.

## Asking Useful Questions

Ask one focused question, then review citations and any evidence gaps before acting. For example:

- “Is checkout-api healthy? Check recent errors and latency.”
- “Which evidence supports the leading hypothesis?”
- “What should I check next to confirm this?”

Keep messages under 4,000 characters. Paste relevant text for analysis; Copilot does not inspect uploaded images or videos. Keep credentials in connector settings rather than chat.

For a complete investigation with a saved report, use [Incident Investigation](/docs/app/investigate/incidents). The [Investigation Map](/docs/app/investigate/incidents#incident-workspace) helps explore relationships between incidents, evidence, and services.
