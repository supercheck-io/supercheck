Ask about your services, errors or logs from **Respond → Copilot**, or from an incident with **Ask Copilot**. Copilot is read-only: it gathers evidence and suggests checks but cannot change your systems.

![Copilot Chat](/screenshots/sre-copilot-chat.png)

## Where to open it

| Entry | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Respond → Copilot** | Full-page chat with history, for general or incident questions |
| **Incident page → ... → Ask Copilot** | Copilot scoped to that incident, opening its most recent chat |

## Chat modes

<Cards className="grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2">
<Card icon={<MessageSquare className="text-blue-500" />} title="General chat">
Name the service to check. Copilot finds sources in your current project and asks if the service is unclear. Connector results are temporary and not added to incident evidence
</Card>
<Card icon={<Siren className="text-red-500" />} title="Incident chat">
Uses the incident's saved evidence, similar past incidents and its linked service's connectors. Starts with three suggested questions; answers cite their evidence. **New chat** keeps the incident context. Results are saved on the incident
</Card>

## Live sources

With **Live sources** on, Copilot can query configured [connectors](/docs/app/investigate/connectors) for fresh data. Turn it off to use only the conversation and saved evidence. If live data is unavailable, check the service and connector configuration in **Services**. A failed or incomplete query is an evidence gap, not a verified result.

## Conversations

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New chat** | Starts a fresh thread. To leave an incident context, open **Respond → Copilot** |
| **History** | Searches and restores your saved general and incident chats |
| **Archive** | Removes a chat from history after you confirm. An archived chat cannot be continued |
| **Chat help** | Shows suggested questions and available sources |

History is private to you within your organization and project. A stopped answer is not saved. **Regenerate** replaces the previous reply; only the new one survives a reload.

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Asking useful questions and limits" id="questions-and-limits">
Ask one focused question, then review citations and evidence gaps before acting.

- "Is checkout-api healthy? Check recent errors and latency."
- "Which evidence supports the leading hypothesis?"
- "What should I check next to confirm this?"
- "What changed before this started?" (incident chat)
- "Have we seen this before?" (incident chat; past incidents may be unrelated)

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| **Message length** | 4,000 characters, applied to the newest message only. Earlier replies in the chat can be longer |
| **Conversation length** | Very long chats drop their oldest turns from what Copilot reads. A chat that is too large is rejected with *Copilot chat history is too large* |
| **Tool calls per reply** | Up to 4. If Copilot still cannot answer, it says *Copilot reached its tool-call limit for this turn.* Ask a narrower question |
| **Rate** | 30 messages per minute per person |
| **Images, videos and files** | Not supported. Paste relevant text instead |

Answers can include markdown tables and small bar, line or area charts (up to 4 series and 48 points) using only values from evidence, tool results or your messages; a chart without numeric values shows as raw data. Keep credentials in connector settings, not in chat. For a saved report, use [Incident investigation](/docs/app/investigate/incidents).

</Accordion>

<Callout type="warn">
On Supercheck Cloud, Copilot replies, incident triage and evidence briefs need an active organization subscription and a valid billing customer; investigation runs also enforce spending limits. If access is blocked, open **Settings → Organization → Subscription**. Self-hosted installations skip these checks.

The free trial includes AI triage and investigations (see [rate limits](/docs/app/investigate/incidents#trial-allowance)). Copilot chat and evidence briefs need a paid plan, and the Copilot screen says so instead of showing the chat.
</Callout>
