Copilot is Supercheck's read-only chat for AI SRE. Use it from **Investigate → Copilot** for open-ended questions, or from the floating panel on an incident page for incident-scoped follow-ups.

Every answer is grounded in evidence Supercheck already owns. Incident chats can also query live read-only connectors when an authorized user opts in.

## What Copilot Reads

| Source | Availability | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| **Stored evidence** | Any incident chat | Native evidence and connector evidence already gathered for the incident, with citations. |
| **Live connectors** | Incident chat with opt-in | Read-only queries to Prometheus, Kubernetes, GitHub, Loki, and other configured connectors. |
| **No tools** | Standalone chat | A chat without an incident cannot inspect evidence and will say so. |

Copilot never writes to your systems and never runs remediation. It gathers evidence, explains what likely happened, and recommends checks you can run yourself.

## Chat Scopes

- **Standalone chat** lives on the Copilot page with its own history sidebar. Use it for open-ended questions that do not need incident evidence.
- **Incident chat** opens from an incident workspace or the floating panel. It is scoped to that incident, verifies the incident before answering, and can use stored evidence and live sources.

Chats can be archived from the history sidebar. Conversations are always scoped to your organization, project, and user.

## Live Sources

Live sources are **off by default**. To use them in an incident chat:

1. The incident must be mapped to a service and have configured connectors.
2. Your role must include connector investigation permission. `project_editor` and above can chat; live sources require the connector permission that viewers and editors do not have by default.
3. Toggle **Live sources** on in the composer before sending.

Each live query is bounded: up to 10 rows, 15 seconds, and a 60 minute time window, with read-only policy checks before anything leaves the platform. Kubernetes queries require an explicit namespace. When a connector is unreachable through a private agent, Copilot tells you the query is pending instead of inventing evidence.

## Limits

- **30 messages per minute** per user. If Redis is unavailable the request fails closed rather than running unthrottled.
- **50 messages** and **20,000 characters** of history per request. Start a new chat when a thread gets long.
- **4 model steps** and a **45 second** timeout per answer. The assistant message is saved when the stream finishes; stopped answers are not saved.

<Callout type="info">
For a full investigation with a saved, shareable report, use [Incident Investigation](/docs/app/investigate/incidents) instead. Copilot is for interactive questions; an investigation runs the complete evidence pass and records its findings on the incident timeline.
</Callout>
