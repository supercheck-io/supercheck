Incidents are the responder queue for issues that need investigation, evidence review, or recovery confirmation.

You can create an incident in two ways:

- From **Communicate → Alerts → Signals**, select **Investigate** next to an alert failure. Repeated deliveries are grouped, and the latest alert opens a new or existing incident without starting a full investigation.
- From **Investigate → Incidents → New incident**, when an issue did not originate from a Supercheck alert.

Both paths open the dedicated incident workspace.

![Create Incident Dialog](/screenshots/create-incident-dialog.png)

## Incident Queue

The incident queue displays active incidents first, ordered by recency. Responders can filter by severity (`SEV1` through `SEV4`), resolution status, or search by title. Quick action buttons provide direct access to **Review alerts** and **Trends**.

![Incident Queue](/screenshots/sre-incidents-queue.png)

## Incident Workspace

Each incident provides a dedicated workspace divided into two functional tabs:

### Overview Tab

The **Overview** tab contains the incident header, metadata, investigation controls, and findings:

- **Run investigation** — Launches a bounded, read-only AI investigation pass against stored evidence and optional live connectors.
- **Include live sources** — Off by default; requires a linked service and connector investigation permissions.
- **Link service & View map** — Connects the incident to a catalog service and visualizes its dependency topology.
- **Save report & feedback** — Responders can snapshot immutable reports and rate findings accuracy.

![Incident Workspace Overview](/screenshots/incident-workspace-overview.png)

Click **View map** to explore the linked service, related alerts, and available evidence. Select a node to inspect its details.

![Investigation Map](/screenshots/sre-investigation-map.png)

After selecting **Run investigation**, follow the status on the page. Wait for completion before saving a report. If it fails or times out, review the error and available evidence before retrying; failed runs cannot be saved as completed reports.

Use **Save report** to keep the findings from a completed investigation. Generate an **Evidence brief** separately when you need a shorter summary to share.

### Evidence & Brief Tab

The **Evidence & brief** tab organizes observations gathered from Supercheck checks and connected external systems.

- **Evidence items** — Detailed observations including HTTP response codes, latency spikes, test assertions, and traces.
- **Evidence brief** — An AI-synthesized, shareable executive summary with findings, hypotheses, and gaps. Responders can regenerate or download the brief as a Markdown file.

![Evidence and Brief](/screenshots/incident-evidence-brief.png)

Select **View map** to explore the incident and its evidence relationships in the Investigation Map:

![Evidence Graph](/screenshots/sre-evidence-graph.png)

### Investigation Report

Reports separate evidence from judgment using explicit **Fact**, **Inference**, and **Hypothesis** labels. The standard responder summary covers:

- **What changed** — Recent code commits, deployments, or configuration updates
- **Blast radius** — Affected endpoints, services, and downstream dependencies
- **Strongest signals** — High-confidence failure patterns and error messages
- **Likely cause & confidence** — Root cause hypothesis with supporting rationale
- **Evidence gaps** — Missing data or unchecked systems
- **Next safe checks** — Recommended read-only commands to verify the state

If available evidence cannot determine root cause, the report explicitly states the gap rather than making assumptions.

### Availability & Permissions

If investigations are disabled, existing evidence and results remain readable, but run and live-source controls are unavailable. Viewers cannot run investigations, use Copilot, or mutate snapshots and feedback. Project Editors can investigate stored evidence and use Copilot; enabling **Live sources** requires connector investigation permission (`project_admin` or higher).

## Incident Trends

Select **Trends** from the incident queue to inspect historical operational metrics over the last 30 days:

- **Volume metrics** — Total created vs. resolved incidents
- **Resolution rate & average resolution time** — Percentage resolved and mean time to resolution
- **Severity mix** — Breakdown across SEV1, SEV2, SEV3, and SEV4
- **Most affected services** — Hotspots requiring architectural remediation

## Copilot Assistance

Copilot is accessible directly from **Investigate → Copilot** as well as the floating launcher on incident pages.

| Mode | Context Available |
|---|---|
| **Standalone** | Responder-provided prompts and questions |
| **Incident-scoped** | Stored native and connector evidence for that specific incident |
| **Incident-scoped with Live Sources** | Bounded connector data queried in real-time when enabled by an authorized role |

Standalone chat does not query infrastructure without context. Incident chat grounds responses in verified observations and will report gaps rather than inventing queries or timestamps.

## Incident Lifecycle & Verification

| Stage | Responsibility | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Triage** | Responder / Supercheck | Select **Investigate** on an alert signal or create an incident manually. High-confidence alerts can auto-correlate to existing incidents. |
| **Investigate** | AI SRE Agent | Reads native evidence and authorized connector sources; generates cited hypotheses. |
| **Decide & Fix** | Human Responder | Evaluates findings and applies necessary remediation in external systems. |
| **Confirm Recovery** | Human Responder | Re-runs synthetic tests, checks monitor status, or observes metrics. |
| **Resolve** | Human Responder | Clicks **Resolve incident**, records fix details and passing checks in the incident timeline. |

Resolving records your decision and the recovery checks you list; it does not run checks or apply remediation. Viewers cannot resolve incidents.

## Alerts vs. Incidents

Alerts and incidents are intentionally separated in Supercheck:

- **Alerts** manage delivery channels, dispatch history, and raw failure signals.
- **Incidents** manage active investigation workflows, evidence collection, and resolution records.

This separation prevents alert noise from cluttering responder queues while ensuring any signal can be escalated with one click.

## Exporting Reports

Download a report or evidence brief to share findings and recommended checks with your team. Review the evidence gaps and any incomplete results before acting on the conclusions. Credentials and raw connector responses are excluded.
