When Supercheck Cloud opens, every new organization starts with a **14-day free trial** of the whole product. No card is required, and nothing is billed during the trial. Self-hosted installations have no trial and no limits.

## What you can use

The trial includes monitors, status pages, Playwright tests, k6 load tests, AI features, and AI SRE. Limits keep it safe for everyone.

| | During the trial |
| --- | --- |
| Monitors | 5, checked at most every 5 minutes |
| Status pages | 2, each with up to 50 subscribers (custom domains are available on paid plans) |
| Playwright | 30 browser minutes in total, 1 run at a time, 5 waiting in the queue |
| k6 load tests | HTTP GET tests of up to 5 virtual users for 5 minutes, against domains you verify |
| AI SRE | 5 full investigations |
| AI credits | 100 |
| Team and projects | 3 team members and 3 projects |

Browser synthetic monitors use the same 30 browser minutes as Playwright tests. When the minutes are used up, those monitors are skipped instead of being reported as down.

## Load testing in a trial

To protect other people's sites, a trial can only run k6 load tests against domains your organization owns.

1. Go to **Admin → Load test domains** and add the domain.
2. Publish the TXT record shown for it in your DNS.
3. Select **Verify**. Supercheck checks the record again each time a test starts.

A trial generates the test script for you from a URL, a user count, and a duration. Custom k6 scripts are available on paid plans.

## See how much is left

A banner shows how many days are left. The **Subscription** tab in Admin shows your remaining AI SRE investigations and AI credits.

## When the trial ends

On the last day, subscribe to Plus or Pro to keep everything running. If you do not:

- Your monitors are **paused** and your status pages are **archived**, so nothing runs without a plan.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Your tests, jobs, monitors, status pages, and history are kept.
- You can still sign in, see your data, and subscribe.

When you subscribe, your paused monitors resume and your archived status pages are republished automatically. Anything you changed yourself in the meantime is left as you set it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I extend the trial?** Contact support. Trial limits can be adjusted for a single organization.

**Does the trial renew?** No. Each organization has one trial.

**Why are my monitors not running after the trial?** They are paused until you subscribe. Choose a plan under **Admin → Subscription**.
