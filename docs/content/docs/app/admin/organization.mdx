## Default organization and projects

Sign-up creates one default organization and one default project. There is no menu to create or switch organizations. Owners and admins rename it with the pencil button (**Rename organization**) next to its name. Create and switch projects with the project selector and the **Projects** tab.

You can accept invitations to projects in other organizations and keep your default one. Those projects appear in the project selector under the team name, and every active project in a joined team is visible read-only. Project Editor and Project Admin assignments add permissions only in the selected projects, and owning your default organization gives no administrative access to another team. Users who sign up through an invitation join the inviting team without a separate organization.

## Projects

Only organization owners and admins create and manage projects in the UI. Project admins can update their assigned projects through the API.

![Projects List](/screenshots/org-admin-projects.png)

<Steps>
<Step>

Open **Organization → Projects**.

</Step>
<Step>

Select **Create Project**, fill in both required fields in the **Create New Project** dialog, then select **Create Project** again.

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Up to 20 characters: letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens, underscores |
| **Description** | Up to 100 characters |

</Step>
</Steps>

![Create Project](/screenshots/org-admin-create-project.png)

Project slugs must be unique. Owners and admins can also delete a project through the API; the default project cannot be deleted.

## Members

Invite teammates and assign [roles](/docs/app/admin/roles-and-permissions).

<Steps>
<Step>
### Invite the member

Open **Organization → Members**, select **Invite Member**, enter the email address, choose a role and send.

![Invite Member Dialog](/screenshots/org-admin-member-invite-dialog.png)

Invitations are limited to 10 per user per hour by default; if the limiter is unavailable they are refused with a retry message. A pending invitation stops working when its sender is removed, demoted from administrator or leaves. Platform administrator accounts cannot accept invitations.
</Step>
<Step>
### The member gets an email

The email contains a link to join your organization.

![Email Invitation](/screenshots/org-admin-member-email-invitation.png)
</Step>
<Step>
### The member accepts

![Accept Invitation](/screenshots/org-admin-member-invite-acceptance.png)
</Step>
<Step>
### The member signs up or signs in

Sign up or sign in with the invited email address, then accept the invitation to reach the assigned projects. On Supercheck Cloud, verify your email from the link we send before joining.

![Invitation Signup](/screenshots/org-admin-member-invite-signup.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Leave an organization" id="leave-an-organization">

Select a project in the team, open your profile menu, choose **Account**, select **Leave** next to the organization and confirm with **Leave organization**. This removes your membership and project assignments in that team; other memberships remain. If none remain, setup creates your personal organization and project. Owners cannot leave; transfer ownership or delete the organization first. An administrator must invite you again to rejoin.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Delete the organization" id="delete-organization">

Only the owner can delete it. Select the trash button (**Delete organization**) next to the organization name, type the organization name and select **Request deletion**. If you signed in a while ago you must sign in again, then reopen the dialog and confirm within a few minutes.

Deletion is never immediate. The organization enters a **pending deletion** window of 14 days (self-hosted installations can change it, see [Self-hosted](/docs/app/deployment/self-hosted)):

- **Read-only.** Every member sees a banner with the date. Members can still view and export, but secret values stay hidden (reveal is refused with `ORG_PENDING_DELETION`), nothing runs, schedules and monitors stop, and nothing can be created, changed or invited.
- **Cancel.** The owner can select **Cancel deletion** in the banner before the date; schedules and monitors resume. Other members see who can cancel.
- **Early purge.** A Super Admin can end the window with **Purge now**, such as for a legal erasure request (see [Super Admin](/docs/app/admin/super-admin)). It can no longer be cancelled after that.
- **Billing.** A subscription stops renewing and ends with the paid period. Cancelling the deletion restores renewal while the period is running; if it ended, subscribe again.
- **Emails.** Owners are emailed when deletion is requested, 7 days and 1 day before, when cancelled, and when complete. Without a mail server (some self-hosted installations) no email is sent and the dialog confirms the date.
- **After the window.** Every project, test, job, run, monitor, status page and stored report is deleted permanently and every member loses access. This cannot be undone.

Audit log entries are kept for 12 months, with email addresses replaced by a hash after 30 days, and then removed. Invoices and payment records stay with the payment provider.

Deletion is postponed, not refused, while the organization has usage that was not billed yet: the purge retries until that usage is settled.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Delete and restore a project" id="delete-project">

Owners and admins delete a project from the **Projects** row menu with **Delete project**, typing the project name. The default project cannot be deleted. Its jobs and monitors stop at once, and the organization's owners and admins are emailed. For 7 days it stays in the list marked **Deleted**, with the restore deadline (hover for the exact time), and an owner or admin can **Restore** it. After that its tests, runs, monitors, status pages and reports are deleted permanently.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## CLI tokens

CLI tokens give project-scoped API access for automation. A token inherits its owner's permissions; use one per pipeline or integration.

<Steps>
<Step>

Open **Organization → CLI Tokens**.

</Step>
<Step>

Select **Create token**. Enter a name unique within the project and, optionally, an expiry date (1 hour to 1 year ahead).

</Step>
<Step>

Copy the generated token and store it securely.

</Step>
</Steps>

A project can have at most 20 active CLI tokens (enabled and not expired). Anyone with token update permission can disable or rename a token, but only the token's owner can re-enable it.

## AI SRE setup

Administrators configure AI SRE from **Integrations** and **Private Agents** ahead of incidents. Register services on **Services** first, then follow [AI SRE setup](/docs/app/admin/ai-sre-setup).

## Audit log

The **Audit** tab records who did what and when. Search entries, or filter by action and user. The table shows the newest 100 events first; **Load older events** fetches the next page. API clients page with the `cursor` parameter of `GET /api/audit`.

![Audit Log](/screenshots/org-admin-audit-list.png)

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Recorded actions" id="audit-actions">

The **Action** filter lists the actions recorded in your log, such as login, project created, or CLI token created.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
