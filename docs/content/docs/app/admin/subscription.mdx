On Supercheck Cloud, open **Settings → Organization → Subscription** to see your organization's plan, usage and estimated bill. Projects in the same organization share allowances; separate organizations do not. Self-hosted deployments have no subscription or usage limits. See [Pricing](/pricing) for Cloud prices, allowances and overage rates.

## What counts toward usage

The table applies to **paid Cloud subscriptions**. Trial allowances last for the trial and have no overage charges; full trial investigations count once started, but failed runs do not. See [Pricing](/pricing) for trial details.

| Usage | How it is measured | After the allowance |
|---|---|---|
| **Playwright** | Execution minutes shared by tests and synthetic monitors | Billed per extra minute |
| **k6** | Peak allocated VUs × run time in minutes, rounded up per run | Billed per extra VU-minute |
| **Full AI SRE investigations** | One unit per successfully completed report | Billed per extra investigation |
| **AI credits** | One credit per accepted generation, fix or failure-analysis action; returned if the AI fails or produces no output | New actions pause; upgrade or wait for renewal |
| **Monitors, projects, status pages** | Number of resources in the organization | Upgrade required |
| **Members** | Members plus pending invitations count toward the limit | Pro: 25 included as standard (a custom plan can include more); the owner buys a total seat count, and each purchased seat above the included members costs $8 per month even when unused, up to 100 members. Plus: upgrade required |

Allowances reset on your monthly billing date and do not roll over. AI SRE work never uses AI credits, and triage, Copilot chat and evidence briefs do not consume full-investigation units. AI credits have no paid overage.

## Review and manage billing

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check the estimate

Review your plan price and measured overages in **Subscription**. Discounts, credits, taxes and plan-change proration can change the final invoice.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set spending controls

Every paid plan starts with a monthly overage cap and a hard stop. The default cap is $70 on Plus and $300 on Pro, so with the defaults a month costs about $99 on Plus or $399 on Pro at most before taxes, plus any extra Pro member seats. Raising the cap or turning off the hard stop allows more overage. Plus with a full cap costs the same as Pro, so a team that often reaches the Plus cap should compare it with Pro.

Owners and organization admins open **Subscription → spending controls** to raise or lower the cap, turn the hard stop off so usage keeps billing past the cap, reset to the plan default, and choose alert thresholds and billing contacts.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add member seats (Pro)

Pro includes 25 members (a custom Pro plan can include more, and that number is then the minimum). The **organization owner** selects **Change member seats** under **Subscription** to set the total number of members, up to 100. Each purchased seat above the included members costs $8 per month, filled or not. The dialog shows the monthly change before you confirm.

- Adding seats charges a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period right away.
- Removing seats takes effect now; the unused time is credited on your next invoice.
- You cannot go below the included members or your current members and pending invitations.
- If the dialog says seats are not available on your subscription yet, contact support.
- Organization admins see the seat count and ask the owner to change it.
- For more than 100 members, contact hello@supercheck.io. Plus organizations upgrade to Pro for more members.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open the billing portal

The **organization owner** selects **Manage Subscription** to view invoices, update payment details or change the subscription. Checkout and resubscription are also owner-only. After a subscription expires, the owner can still open the portal to view past invoices; this does not restore paid features.

Seat changes are made only with **Change member seats**, and plan changes only through **Manage Subscription**. A refund does not change your plan by itself; access follows the subscription's status, so a refunded subscription that is cancelled or revoked loses paid access.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="What happens at the cap">
With the hard stop on, new browser (Playwright) checks, test and job runs, k6 load tests and full AI SRE investigations pause when the cap is reached. They resume when you raise the cap or your billing period resets. HTTP, website, ping, port and MCP monitors keep running. Emails go out at 80% of the cap (and 90% when the hard stop is on) and at 100%. The subscription fee is separate. Work already admitted or running can still accrue usage, so the cap is not an exact limit on your final invoice.
</Callout>

## Plan changes and cancellation

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="After moving to a plan with lower limits" id="lower-limits">

The newest monitors and published status pages above the new limits are paused or archived by a limit check that runs every 15 minutes. It can take several runs when many organizations change plan at once; nothing is deleted. To turn one back on, first free a slot (pause a monitor or unpublish a page) or upgrade, because the organization is then at its limit.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Without a paid plan or active trial" id="no-plan">

The organization cannot declare incidents or schedule, change or extend maintenance windows, and an incident it opens does not start an automatic AI investigation. Viewing, acknowledging and resolving incidents and ending or cancelling windows still work.

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Deleting an organization" id="deleting-an-organization">

Requesting deletion stops the subscription from renewing, and usage so far is still billed. The subscription ends with the paid period, or at the purge if that comes first: when the deletion window ends before the paid period does, or a Super Admin purges the organization early, the remaining paid time is not used.

Cancelling the deletion turns renewal back on if the period is still running and the deletion request turned it off; if you had turned renewal off yourself, it stays off. If the billing provider is unavailable at that moment, renewal is turned back on once it responds. If the period already ended, subscribe again. Self-hosted installations have no billing.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
