On Supercheck Cloud, open **Settings → Organization → Subscription** to see your organization's plan, usage, and estimated bill. Projects in the same organization share allowances; separate organizations do not.

<Callout type="info">
Subscriptions apply to **Supercheck Cloud**. Self-hosted deployments have no subscription or usage limits. See [Pricing](/pricing) for current Cloud prices, allowances, and overage rates.
</Callout>

## What counts toward usage?

The table below applies to **paid Cloud subscriptions**. Trial allowances last for the trial and have no overage charges; full trial investigations count once started, but failed runs do not. See [Pricing](/pricing) for trial details.

| Usage | How it is measured | After the allowance |
|---|---|---|
| **Playwright** | Execution minutes shared by tests and synthetic monitors | Billed per extra minute |
| **k6** | Peak allocated VUs × run time in minutes, rounded up per run | Billed per extra VU-minute |
| **Full AI SRE investigations** | One unit per successfully completed report | Billed per extra investigation |
| **AI credits** | One credit per accepted generation, fix, or failure-analysis action; returned if the AI fails or produces no output | New actions pause; upgrade or wait for renewal |
| **Monitors, projects, status pages** | Number of resources in the organization | Upgrade required |
| **Members** | Members plus pending invitations | Pro: 25 included, then $8 per extra member per month, up to 100 members. Plus: upgrade required |

Allowances reset on your monthly billing date and do not roll over. AI SRE work never uses AI credits, and triage, Copilot chat, and evidence briefs do not consume full-investigation units. AI credits have no paid overage.

## Review and manage billing

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check the estimate

Review your plan price and measured overages in **Subscription**. Discounts, credits, taxes, and plan-change proration can change the final invoice.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set spending controls

Every paid plan starts with a monthly overage cap and a hard stop. The default is $70 on Plus and $300 on Pro, so with the default cap and hard stop a month costs about $99 on Plus or $399 on Pro at most before taxes. Raising the cap or turning off the hard stop allows more overage. Plus with a full cap costs the same as Pro, so a team that often reaches the Plus cap should compare it with Pro.

Owners and organization admins can open **Subscription → spending controls** to raise or lower the cap, turn the hard stop off so usage keeps billing past the cap, reset to the plan default, and choose alert thresholds and billing contacts.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add member seats (Pro)

Pro includes 25 team members. The **organization owner** can select **Change member seats** under **Subscription** to set the total number of members, up to 100. Each member above 25 costs $8 per month. The dialog shows the monthly change before you confirm. Adding seats charges a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period right away. Removing seats takes effect now, and the unused time is credited on your next invoice. You can't go below 25 or below your current members and pending invitations. Organization admins see the seat count and ask the owner to change it. Need more than 100 members? Contact us at hello@supercheck.io. Plus organizations upgrade to Pro for more members.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open the billing portal

The **organization owner** selects **Manage Subscription** to view invoices, update payment details, or change the subscription. After a move to a plan with lower limits, the newest monitors and published status pages above the new limits are paused or archived by a limit check that runs every 15 minutes. It can take several runs when many organizations change plan at once; nothing is deleted. To turn one back on, first free a slot (pause a monitor or unpublish a page) or upgrade, because the organization is then at its limit. Checkout and resubscription are also owner-only. The owner can still open the portal to view past invoices after a subscription expires; this does not restore paid features.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="What happens at the cap">
With the hard stop on, new browser (Playwright) checks, test and job runs, k6 load tests, and full AI SRE investigations pause when the cap is reached. They resume when you raise the cap or your billing period resets. HTTP, website, ping, and port monitors keep running. Emails go out at 80% of the cap (and 90% when the hard stop is on) and at 100%. The subscription fee is separate. Work already admitted or running can still accrue usage, so the cap is not an exact limit on your final invoice.
</Callout>
