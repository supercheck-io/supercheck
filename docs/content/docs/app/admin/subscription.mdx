Open **Admin → Subscription** to see your organization's plan, usage, and estimated bill. Projects in the same organization share allowances; separate organizations do not.

<Callout type="info">
Subscriptions apply to **Supercheck Cloud**. Self-hosted deployments have no subscription or usage limits. See [Pricing](/pricing) for current Cloud prices, allowances, and overage rates.
</Callout>

## What counts toward usage?

| Usage | How it is measured | After the allowance |
|---|---|---|
| **Playwright** | Execution minutes shared by tests and synthetic monitors | Billed per extra minute |
| **k6** | Peak allocated VUs × run time in minutes, rounded up per run | Billed per extra VU-minute |
| **Full AI SRE investigations** | One unit per successfully completed report | Billed per extra investigation |
| **AI credits** | One credit per accepted generation, fix, or failure-analysis action, including actions that later fail | New actions pause; upgrade or wait for renewal |
| **Monitors, projects, status pages, members** | Number of resources in the organization | Upgrade required |

Allowances reset on your monthly billing date and do not roll over. AI SRE triage, Copilot chat, and evidence briefs do not consume full-investigation units. AI credits have no paid overage.

## Review and manage billing

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check the estimate

Review your plan price and measured overages in **Subscription**. Discounts, credits, taxes, and plan-change proration can change the final invoice.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set spending controls

Configure overage alerts and an optional spending limit. Owners and organization admins can review usage and update billing settings.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open the billing portal

The **organization owner** selects **Manage Subscription** to view Polar invoices, update payment details, or change the subscription. Checkout and resubscription are also owner-only.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Spending limits cover overages">
The subscription fee is separate. Optional blocking stops new billable executions and full investigations after the limit is reached; basic uptime checks continue. Work already admitted or running can still accrue usage. The limit is not a guaranteed cap on your final invoice.
</Callout>

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Can I view invoices after the subscription expires?">
The owner can still open the portal if the organization retains its Polar billing customer. Paid feature access follows subscription status and period end; opening the portal does not restore an expired subscription.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
