# Supercheck trademark policy

The Supercheck name and logo identify the Supercheck project and its official
hosted service. The maintainers are their current stewards. This policy does not
claim a registered trademark or change any software license.

You may use the name to identify the software you use, describe compatibility,
link to the project, or accurately say “based on Supercheck.” Unmodified
deployments may identify the installed software as Supercheck. Make clear who
operates your deployment or service.

Do not use the name, logo, or a confusingly similar domain or product name to
imply that a fork, hosted service, integration, or organization is official,
endorsed, or operated by Supercheck. Use your own service or product identity
for such offerings. Permission to use the logo as your product branding
requires written approval from hello@supercheck.io.

The domain supercheck.io identifies the official project website; owning a
domain is separate from having a registered trademark. Copyright, license,
and third-party attribution notices must remain where their licenses require
them. This policy does not require removal of those notices or restrict rights
granted by the open-source licenses.
